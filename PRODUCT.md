# PRODUCT.md: Detent

## Register

brand. The marketing site, launch funnel and store ARE the product experience. Commerce surfaces (shop, cart) lean toward product-register clarity inside the brand shell.

## Platform

web

## What it is

**Detent One** is a desktop dial machined from one billet of 6061 aluminum with **software-defined haptics**. A brushless gimbal motor and a 14-bit magnetic encoder let firmware decide how the knob feels, per app and in about a millisecond: 24 crisp clicks for frame-stepping, a weighted fluid glide for scrubbing, a spring that pulls back to center for shuttle, hard end stops for volume, or **Magnet**, which snaps to the things that matter (markers, keyframes, diff hunks). A round AMOLED display in the knob face shows what it is controlling. USB-C and Bluetooth, local-first, open SDK, open firmware.

Price: **$349** (Raw, Graphite, Glacier). **Founders Edition $449**: Tally red, numbered 0001–2000, walnut plinth. Launch price $299 for reservations and launch week. 60-day studio trial, 3-year warranty, free shipping.

Detent is a **concept product**; the site is a design and engineering demonstration and says so plainly (footer and every form). Nothing is sold and no personal data leaves the browser.

## Who it's for

People who make things on a screen for hours and whose hands are bored or overworked: **video editors and colorists, music producers and mix engineers, designers and 3D artists, developers**. They already own a good keyboard, a good mouse, maybe a Stream Deck. They pay for tools that make them faster and that feel good to touch (a $300 mechanical keyboard, a $250 mouse, Teenage Engineering gear). They are skeptical of gadgets and allergic to marketing fluff; they trust specs, demos, and other makers.

## Purpose of the site

1. **Make people feel it through the screen.** The dial on the page is interactive, clicks audibly (opt-in), vibrates on Android, and makes the page respond. If a visitor turns it once, they understand the product.
2. **Justify the premium** with materials, mechanism, specs, openness, warranty and trial: evidence, not adjectives.
3. **Convert by launch phase:** tease (email) → waitlist (referrals) → reserve ($20 refundable deposit) → launch (order) → live store. One config value moves every CTA.
4. **Spread:** the safecracker challenge, shareable waitlist cards, the feel library and the dial toy are built to be screen-recorded and posted.

## Positioning

Not a "macro pad" and not a "stream controller". Detent is **an instrument for software**: the first input device whose *feel* changes with what you're doing. The competitor sentence we refuse: "A premium customizable controller for creators with beautiful design." Ours: "A knob that clicks one frame at a time in Resolve, stops dead at 0 dB in Logic, and snaps to every hunk in your diff."

## Brand personality

Three physical-object words: **machined, exacting, playful**.
- Machined: the precision of a watch crown, a lab instrument, a good camera dial. Numbers are exact (0.022°, 412 g, 32 mN·m).
- Exacting: short declarative sentences. Specific verbs. No hype words ("revolutionary", "seamless", "unleash", "elevate", "game-changer").
- Playful: the dial is a toy. The site rewards curiosity (hidden safecracker, feel library, sound). Humor is dry and rare.

Voice: a senior engineer who is also a musician explaining their favorite tool to a friend. Confident, concrete, a little wry.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages: hero-metric templates, three-card icon grids, gradient blobs, purple gradients, glass cards.
- Apple pastiche: huge centered product shot + "Pro. Beyond." copy + silver everything.
- Teenage Engineering pastiche: orange-and-gray, fake-industrial mono labels everywhere. (We admire it; we must not look like it.)
- "Gamer RGB" controller sites.
- Editorial-magazine lane (italic display serifs, broadsheet rules): wrong register for an instrument.
- Long preloaders, scroll-jacking, cursor followers that hide the real cursor.

## Strategic design principles

1. **Show the mechanism, then the feeling.** Every claim is demonstrable on the page (turn it, hear it, see the physics).
2. **The page is the product demo.** The dial controls things on the site; interaction teaches faster than copy.
3. **Evidence over adjectives.** Specs, materials, numbers, open source, repairability, warranty.
4. **Honest urgency only.** Real batch dates, a real numbered run, a deposit that is refundable. No fake timers or fake stock counters.
5. **Fast is premium.** LCP < 2.0 s on 4G mid-range phones; 3D loads after first paint and never blocks reading; everything works without WebGL.
6. **Delight rewards attention.** Hidden things for people who poke around; nothing that blocks people who don't.

## Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA minimum. Every interactive dial is a keyboard-operable ARIA slider. Sound is opt-in and never required. `prefers-reduced-motion` gets calm crossfades and no autorotation. Color is never the only signal (profile names accompany profile colors). Touch targets ≥ 44 px. Focus is always visible (tally ring).

## Conversion & proof

- Primary conversion per phase: email (tease), referral joins (waitlist), $20 deposit (reserve), order (launch/live).
- Proof we can honestly show: specs, materials, mechanism diagrams, open SDK/firmware, warranty/trial terms, early-tester notes (fictional personas, clearly within a concept site), integrations list with concrete behaviors.
- Risk reversal: fully refundable deposit, 60-day trial, 3-year warranty, free returns, repairable with four screws.
